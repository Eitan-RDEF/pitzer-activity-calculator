Frequently asked questions

About the online Pitzer calculator.

What is the Pitzer activity model?

The Pitzer model represents non-ideal interactions in electrolyte solutions and is particularly useful at ionic strengths where simpler Debye–Hückel or Davies approaches become inadequate.

What information do I enter?

Enter analytical component totals in mol/kg H₂O or mmol/kg H₂O, together with known pH and temperature. Component totals are not free-ion concentrations; PHREEQC calculates the equilibrium aqueous species distribution.

Does the calculator determine pH automatically?

No. The current workflow requires a known pH on the hydrogen-ion activity basis. Calculated-pH and atmospheric CO₂-equilibrium modes are intentionally outside its scope.

How has the calculator been validated?

Selected mean activity coefficients and osmotic coefficients have been compared with published NIST ThermoML data, while concentrated implementation cases have been compared with published USGS PHRQPITZ results. The evidence applies only to the documented cases and conditions.

Can it calculate precipitation, gases or redox equilibrium?

No. The application calculates a closed aqueous system at fixed pressure. It does not equilibrate minerals or gases, calculate redox state, or alter inputs to force charge balance.

Is the calculator free to use?

Yes. The browser calculator is free to use and the application source is released under the MIT License. Bundled scientific software and reference data retain their respective notices and terms.
